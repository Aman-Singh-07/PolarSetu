# DEMO FLOW

Target demo duration: ~4-5 minutes.

## Opening (20-30 sec)
"Polar science produces reports, datasets, publications and media. NCPOR already operates specialist information systems for these resources. Our solution is the layer that connects discovery, evidence and outreach into one user experience."

## Scene 1 — Discover (40 sec)
1. Search for a real polar topic such as "sea ice".
2. Show filters and a result set.
3. Open one resource and point to its metadata and official source.

## Scene 2 — Connect (40 sec)
1. Open an expedition page.
2. Show the map, timeline and related evidence.
3. Explain that the resource relationship model connects the expedition to reports, datasets, publications and media.

## Scene 3 — Understand (60 sec)
1. Ask: "What did this expedition study?"
2. Show an answer with visible source cards.
3. Ask something outside the corpus and show the system explicitly refusing to invent a source-based answer.

## Scene 4 — Create (60 sec)
1. Select a report -> open Polar Outreach Studio.
2. Choose "Student" audience -> Generate Student Explanation.
3. Generate a social or website draft from the same source set.
4. Emphasize that it is marked `DRAFT — human review required`.

## Scene 5 — Govern (40 sec)
1. Open review queue.
2. Show DRAFT status and source IDs.
3. Approve it. Explain that official content is not auto-published in the MVP.

## Closing (20-30 sec)
"PolarSetu is not another isolated archive or chatbot. It is a source-aware discovery and dissemination layer that makes existing polar knowledge easier to find, understand, connect and responsibly share."
