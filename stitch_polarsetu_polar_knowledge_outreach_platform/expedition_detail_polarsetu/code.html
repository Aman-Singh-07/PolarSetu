Executive Summary & Objectives

Ref: MoES/NCPOR/43-ISEA

The 43rd Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica concluded its 134-day campaign spanning Bharati Station (Larsemann Hills) and Maitri Station (Schirmacher Oasis). Scientific tasks concentrated on long-term climate monitoring, subglacial hydrology, ozone layer profiling, and cryospheric microplastic signatures in fast-ice zones of Prydz Bay.

air Atmospheric & Ozone Profiling

Continuous ozonesonde balloon ascents deployed to monitor the spring recovery anomaly over Queen Maud Land.

hardware Larsemann Ice-Shelf Drilling

Retrieved three pristine 45-meter firn/ice cores to reconstruct the past 180 years of Southern Ocean aerosol deposition.

microbiology Permafrost Microbial Biota

Sampled benthic mats from 8 proglacial lakes in Schirmacher Oasis to isolate novel psychrotolerant biocatalysts.

sensors High-Altitude AWS Telemetry

Commissioned two hardened autonomous weather telemetry stations on the polar plateau relaying via INSAT-3DR.

PUB-2024-082 • Fast-Ice Marine Ecology
verified DOI Catalogued

Prydz Bay Sea Ice Microplastic Distribution & Cryo-Concentration

Analysis of 16 fast-ice cores retrieved 12 km offshore Bharati Station established baseline concentrations of fibrous nano-polymers (avg 4.2 particles/L), correlated with seasonal sea-ice brine rejection dynamics.

Lead Author: Dr. S. Mukherjee et al. (NCPOR & NIO) View Full Paper & Fast-Ice Spectrograms arrow_forward Prototype demonstration data
NPDC-DATA-411 • Glacial Radar Survey
database NetCDF Archive

Sub-glacial Hydrology & Channel Network Mapping at Schirmacher Oasis

Multi-frequency Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) mapped 14 active subglacial melt channels draining towards the shelf break, revealing unexpected geothermal temperature gradients near Zub Lake.

Coverage: 420 line-km radar transects Access Hydro-Model Raster Datasets download Prototype demonstration data
RPT-43-BIO • Extremophile Genomics
biotech GenBank Registered

Microbial Extremophiles & Cold-Active Enzymes in Lake Priyadarshini

Isolation and complete genome sequencing of four psychrotolerant bacterial strains (genera Psychrobacter and Flavobacterium) exhibiting lipase activity at sub-zero temperatures.
