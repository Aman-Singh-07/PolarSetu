Journal of Polar Marine Biology (Elsevier) • Vol. 48, Iss. 3, 2024

Quantifies chlorophyll-a irradiance triggers beneath 1.8-meter seasonal fast ice in Prydz Bay. Demonstrates earlier photoprotective pigment activation in diatom communities driven by thinning sub-surface snow covers during anomalous warm air events.

MoES Polar Data Stewardship Policy Notice

In compliance with the Antarctic Treaty System and Government of India Open Geospatial Data Directives, raw observations gathered by national polar expeditions are placed in the public domain post standard 18-month embargo periods. Citations must reference NCPOR grant identifiers and assign proper researcher attribution.
