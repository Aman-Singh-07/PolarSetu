Grounded Response Query: What were the major findings related to Antarctic sea ice dynamics?
verified 100% Provenance Matched

Based on 3 catalogued expedition reports and 2 NPDC datasets from the 42nd and 43rd Indian Scientific Expeditions to Antarctica (ISEA), significant findings include:

1. Multi-Year Fast Ice Thinning Source: RPT-43-ICE-02

Average fast-ice thickness in Prydz Bay exhibited a 14.2% seasonal thinning between November 2023 and February 2024, attributed to intensified oceanic heat flux from modified Circumpolar Deep Water.

Fast-ice depth: 1.82m → 1.56m Station: Bharati Coastline Confidence: 99.4%
2. Microalgal Biomass Accumulation Source: PUB-2024-082

Sub-ice chlorophyll-a blooms peaked 18 days earlier than the 10-year decadal baseline, indicating earlier spring solar penetration through reduced snowpack.

Decadal peak: Dec 12 → Nov 24 Spectral Radiance: +22 µmol photons m⁻²s⁻¹
3. Cryospheric Salt Flux Dataset: NPDC-DS-8821

Brine rejection profiling during initial autumn freeze-up showed high salinity anomalies (34.8 PSU) in the upper 50m water column, confirming heightened convective overturning near the Amery Ice Shelf front.

Sensor: CTD Rosette Cast #14 NPDC Quality Flag: Passed (Tier-1)
