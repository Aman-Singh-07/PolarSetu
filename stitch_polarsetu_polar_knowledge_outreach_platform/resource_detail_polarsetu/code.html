ISEA-43 Glaciology & Sea-Ice Core Mechanics Technical Report (Vol. 4)

Comprehensive fast-ice thickness, brine volume fraction, and sub-surface temperature gradients observed along Prydz Bay and Bharati Station coastal transects during the 43rd Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica.

Field Operation Record • Station Sector 4-B

Dr. Anand Sharma and NCPOR glaciology team retrieving a 1.84m intact sea-ice core along coastal fast-ice transect T-04, Prydz Bay, Larsemann Hills.

Abstract & Executive Summary

Section 1.0 • Peer-Reviewed

During the 43rd Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica (ISEA-43, Austral Summer 2023–2024), continuous thermodynamic profiling and mechanical extraction of coastal fast ice were conducted across a 32-kilometer transect originating from Bharati Station in Prydz Bay. Over a 134-day observation window, sixteen (16) intact vertical ice cores measuring up to 2.12 meters were harvested using non-destructive fiberglass coring heads.

In-situ crystallographic analysis was correlated with dual-frequency ground-penetrating radar (GPR) sounding at 400 MHz and 900 MHz to delineate seasonal snow-ice interfaces, sub-surface brine pockets, and basal ablation zones. Thermodynamic model inversions demonstrate accelerated basal shear softening driven by warm water intrusions into the Quilty Bay embayment, exhibiting anomalous latent heat dissipation throughout January 2024.

Methodology & Instrumentation

Data collection adhered to Scientific Committee on Antarctic Research (SCAR) standards. Field instrumentation and analytical procedures included:

radar
Dual-Frequency Ground Radar MALA ProEx GPR equipped with shielded 400 MHz and 900 MHz antennas mounted on low-friction HDPE sledges.
cyclone
Kovacs Cryo-Drill System 9cm internal diameter Mark II ice corer powered by high-torque electric drive to ensure minimal thermal friction.
device_thermostat
Platinum RTD Needle Probes Sub-surface temperature measured within 90 seconds of extraction at 5cm spacing intervals via Fluke 5626 calibration sensors.
water_drop
Sea-Bird CTD Hydrographic Profiler SBE 19plus V2 SeaCAT Cast deployed across drilled core orifices to capture boundary layer oceanic salinity & thermocline depth.
