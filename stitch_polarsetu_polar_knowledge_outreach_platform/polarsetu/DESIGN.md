## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authority of national research infrastructure and the clarity of a high-echelon digital museum. It bridges deep polar oceanographic and cryospheric science with universal accessibility, balancing the academic rigor of India's Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES / NCPOR) with clean, engaging public science outreach.

The visual aesthetic operates at the intersection of **Corporate / Modern** institutional clarity and **Refined Scientific Minimalism**. It avoids gamification, loud neon glows, or pseudo-futuristic ornamentation in favor of precision, high-contrast legibility, tactile verification states, and grounded cryospheric themes. The atmosphere evokes the stillness of polar midnight, crisp ice shelves, and controlled laboratory inquiry. Every layout element prioritizes provenance, academic confidence, and immediate visual verification.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of Arctic and Antarctic exploration governed by institutional protocol:

- **Primary (`#0B132B`)**: Deep polar midnight navy. Used for main headings, primary text, high-level navigation chrome, and authoritative structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`)**: Glacial azure. Acts as the primary active action tone, interactive links, primary buttons, and selected states.
- **Tertiary (`#065F46`)**: Subdued aurora pine. Serves scientific metadata verification, peer-reviewed badges, and confirmed expedition datasets.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate gray. Calibrated for secondary metadata, subtle borders, descriptive captions, and inactive structural indicators.

### Named Colors & Semantic Applications
- `polar-navy-surface` (`#1C2541`): Used for elevated dark-canvas panels, research portal topbars, and deep telemetry headers.
- `ice-white` (`#F8FAFC`): The primary background canvas, avoiding clinical glare while providing a cool, polar-paper contrast.
- `slate-border` (`#E2E8F0`) and `slate-border-strong` (`#CBD5E1`): Define structured data grids, document splitters, and card envelopes without heavy dropshadows.
- `aurora-emerald` (`#10B981`) and `aurora-cyan` (`#06B6D4`): Subtle status accents for live sensor feeds, active telemetry, and data provenance chips.
- `draft-amber-*`: Explicitly reserved for unverified AI-generated summaries and automated social drafts (`DRAFT — human review required`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display and section headings with **Inter** for long-form reading, institutional reports, tabular data, and metadata badges.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a disciplined yet contemporary character. Its crisp geometry gives national scientific research initiatives an approachable, high-trust tone without appearing bureaucratic or outdated.
- **Inter** handles high-density expedition data, research abstracts, scientific attribution tables, and UI controls. Its tall x-height and neutral proportions maintain pristine legibility under complex multi-column dashboard constraints.
- **Label Tokens** are tightly tracked and capitalized when paired with `label-sm` to serve as unambiguous catalog and classification signifiers (e.g., station coordinates, MoES grant IDs, and provenance references).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with strict max-width constraints (1360px) to prevent scientific documentation and horizontal data tables from over-stretching on ultrawide monitors.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rules
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Full 12-column grid. Outer page margin set to `margin` (2rem / 32px), column gutter set to `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Analytical sidebars occupy 3 or 4 columns; primary scientific dossiers occupy the remaining 8 or 9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Metadata sidebars shift into modular expandable accordion trays or stacked summary blocks above the main content stream.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column flow. Outer canvas margin contracts to `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px) and gutters compress to `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Secondary telemetry panels stack below primary findings.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal component padding operates on a predictable 4px/8px modular rhythm. Dense information surfaces (such as data tables and dataset cards) use `space-sm` and `space-md` for high spatial efficiency, while narrative outreach pages utilize `space-lg` and `space-xl` to give expedition photography and executive summaries visual breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **crisp structural borders and tonal layering** rather than exaggerated drop shadows. The design system simulates depth through architectural stratification, mirroring geological and ice-core strata.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0)**: Set to `ice-white` (`#F8FAFC`). Provides a calm, low-glare ground for all modules.
- **Card & Repository Surfaces (Level 1)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `slate-border` (`#E2E8F0`). No default shadow; separation is achieved purely through value contrast.
- **Elevated / Interactive Overlays (Level 2)**: Used for active modals, dropdowns, and pinned source citations. Rendered in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `slate-border-strong` (`#CBD5E1`) and an ambient, low-contrast diffuse shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.06)`).
- **Deep Anchors (Level 3)**: Institutional navigation, interactive map controls, and terminal headers utilize `polar-midnight-deep` (`#0B132B`) with internal contrast lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (4px base, 8px for containers, 12px for larger modal windows). 

This restrained geometry preserves institutional authority and precision, avoiding the overly playful or casual impression of heavily rounded interfaces. Sharp corners are softened just enough to ensure human ergonomics across dense interactive dashboards, while metadata tags, code snippets, and verification badges maintain tight, technical silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `polar-midnight-deep` (`#0B132B`) or `secondary` (`#0284C7`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with `font-medium` (14px). Rounded at 4px (`rounded-md`). Hover state introduces subtle surface brightness (+5%) and active state scales slightly to 98% with an outline ring.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` background with a 1px `slate-border` (`#E2E8F0`) border and `polar-midnight-deep` text. Hover changes border to `slate-border-strong` and background to `ice-white`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button**: Transparent background, text colored in `#0284C7` or `#64748B`, with an inline icon (Lucide).

### Scientific Metadata Badges & Chips
- **Status & Peer Review Badges**: Compact 11px uppercase typography (`label-sm`). 
- **Peer-Reviewed Verified**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
- **Dataset / Geo Coordinates**: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Mandatory Review Banner**: A fixed amber alert badge for AI-synthesized texts (`background: #FEF3C7; color: #92400E; border: 1px solid #F59E0B; font-weight: 600`) bearing the permanent label `DRAFT — human review required`.

### Cards & Repositories
- **Research Dossier Card**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Cards feature a structured top metadata row (expedition date, station tag, MoES grant reference), followed by a `Plus Jakarta Sans` headline, body abstract, and grounded action footer.
- **Grounding Source Card**: Sub-cards anchored directly below AI-assisted responses. Framed in `#F8FAFC` with a left border-accent in `#0284C7` (2px). Displays publication DOI, citation title, specific page numbers, and direct data repository links.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px vertical padding, and 12px horizontal padding. Focus state features a 2px outer ring in `#0EA5E9` with 0px offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square/circle with a 1.5px `#94A3B8` border. Checked state fills with `#0284C7` featuring a clean white checkmark/bullet.

### Data Lists & Tables
- **Institutional Tables**: Zero-shadow tables with full-width horizontal rules in `#E2E8F0`. Header row uses `ice-white` (`#F8FAFC`) with tracked `label-sm` uppercase text in `#475569`. Rows alternate with subtle hover highlighting (`#F8FAFC`) to facilitate horizontal scanning across dense scientific metrics.
