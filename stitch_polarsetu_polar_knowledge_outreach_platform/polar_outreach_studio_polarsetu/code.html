How Antarctic Sea Ice Acts as the Earth's Giant Sunshield

Imagine a giant white mirror floating on the ocean that bounces sunlight back into space. Indian scientists at Bharati station recently discovered that sea ice in Prydz Bay is melting 18 days earlier than usual, reducing the ice-albedo effect that cools our planet.
